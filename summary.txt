## 5. FINAL ASSESSMENT
- **Normalized CSAT (0–10):** 7.3
- **Executive Summary:** The call was an inbound support/complaint call where the customer expressed dissatisfaction with past service issues and sought a monthly payment plan for renewal. While the agent professionally acknowledged past issues and offered a reasonable alternative (extended plan duration), the customer's primary request for monthly payment was denied due to company policy. The call concluded with a clear follow-up plan, but the customer remained hesitant about the proposed long-term commitment.
- **Actionable Coaching Tip:** The agent could explore if there are any *short-term* trial or introductory optimization plans (even if not full service) that could align with the customer's desire to "see sales performance" before committing to a longer duration, rather than immediately pushing for a 3-month commitment. This might bridge the gap between company policy and customer's immediate need for proof of concept.
